**Pak Rat** is Leaf's on-device store. Some of what it carries are apps, which
launch from the **Apps** tab. The rest add a system or an emulator choice to your
library. For the apps that come with Leaf, see
[Built-in apps](/apps/introduction/). Pak Rat carries themes too, covered in
[Themes](/guide/pak-rat-themes/).

## Systems and emulators

- [Flycastrophe](/app-store/flycastrophe/) - a faster core for Dreamcast, Naomi,
  and Atomiswave games.
- [J2ME](/app-store/j2me/) - a system for Java phone games.
- [PICO-8 for Leaf](/guide/pico-8/) - browse Splore and play PICO-8 carts
  natively with the runtime you buy from Lexaloffle.
- [ScummVM](/app-store/scummvm/) - a system for point-and-click adventures.
- [Yabadabadoo](/app-store/yabadabadoo/) - an experimental alternate Saturn core.
- DSperate - two more Nintendo DS cores, covered under Nintendo DS in
  [Systems and emulators](/guide/systems/).

## More games

- [Itch.io](/app-store/itchio/) - browse compatible homebrew and add games or
  soundtracks directly to Leaf.
- [PortMaster](/app-store/portmaster/) - an unofficial PortMaster integration for
  installing compatible native game ports.

## Media

- [Disco Boy](/app-store/disco-boy/) - a music player.
- [SDLReader](/app-store/sdlreader/) - a reader for PDFs, comics, ebooks, and
  text.
- [Video From Hell](/app-store/video-from-hell/) - a video player for films on
  your SD cards and your recorded gameplay.

## Tools

- [Nimbus](/app-store/nimbus/) - a weather app.
- [RAOfflineProxy](/app-store/raofflineproxy/) - earn casual RetroAchievements
  offline and send them when you reconnect.
- [Syncthing](/app-store/syncthing/) - keep game saves and optional save states
  in sync with your other devices.

## Installing an app

Open **System** with the **MENU** button, go to **Actions**, then choose
**Pak Rat**. Pick what you want and install it over Wi-Fi.

An app appears in the **Apps** tab and launches like any game. A new system
appears with your other systems. A new emulator appears under **Core** in the
options of the systems and games it can run.

Pak Rat only offers what your Leaf can run. If something on this page is missing
from Pak Rat, [update Leaf](/guide/updating/).

### Installing without Wi-Fi

Where an app's page links its releases page under Credits, you can install it
by hand. Download the app's `.pak.zip` on a computer, unzip it, copy the `.pak`
folder into `Apps/mlp1/` on the SD card, then run **System > Rescan Library**.

## Removing a pak

Removing a pak that added a system or an emulator takes that system or emulator
away on the next rescan. Your files under `Roms/`, `Images/`, `BIOS/`, `Saves/`,
and `States/` stay on the card.
