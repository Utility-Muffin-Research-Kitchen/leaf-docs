Mock up a handheld in colours the manufacturer never sold. Every studio composites real
renders or product photos, so what you see is the actual device wearing the parts you
picked - not an approximation. Each build lives in its share link, so you can paste one
and it opens exactly as you left it.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Miniloong Pocket 1" icon="seti:image">
    Tint the transparent faceplate to any colour, pick a white, black, or retro body, and
    put a custom firmware logo on the screen - AURKNIX, Bloom, dArkOS, Knulli, or Leaf.

    [Open the studio](/mlp1-colorways/) · [Buy the device](https://www.aliexpress.us/w/wholesale-MINILOONG-POCKET-1.html?g=y&SearchText=MINILOONG+POCKET+1&sortType=price_asc)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Retroid Pocket Nova" icon="seti:image">
    Seven colourways across the body and back shell, the D-pad, A/B/X/Y, all four function
    buttons, both sticks and their RGB LED rings. Switch between stylised renders and real
    product photos, including a drag-to-flip 3D view.

    [Open the studio](/nova-colorways/) · [Buy the device](https://www.goretroid.com/products/retroid-pocket-nova-handheld)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Mangmi Air X" icon="seti:image">
    Front and back. Mix the D-pad, A/B/X/Y, both button pairs, the bumpers, the triggers
    and the power and volume keys across three colourways, cap each stick in black or
    white, and swap the wallpaper.

    [Open the studio](/mangmi-colorways/) · [Buy the device](https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-air-x)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Mangmi Pocket Max" icon="seti:image">
    Pick a body, recolour the D-pad and the A/B/X/Y set, and choose one of three
    wallpapers.

    [Open the studio](/mangmi-colorways/?d=max) · [Buy the device](https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-pocket-max)
  </Card>

  <Card title="NextUI palettes" icon="seti:image">
    Not a colorway but the same idea, for software: design a NextUI theme with a colour
    wheel and watch it land on a TrimUI Brick, Brick Hammer, Smart Pro or Smart Pro S,
    then download the palette file.

    [Open the studio](/nextui-palettes/) · [Buy a device](https://www.trimuistore.com/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
