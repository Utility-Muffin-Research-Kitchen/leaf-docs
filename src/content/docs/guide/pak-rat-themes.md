---
title: Themes
description: Pick a theme for Grid view and Cover Flow, and install, update or remove community themes from Pak Rat over Wi-Fi.
---

A theme restyles Grid view and Cover Flow with its own wallpaper, colors and system
art. Leaf comes with one, **Sample**, and you can install more over Wi-Fi from
**Pak Rat**, Leaf's on-device store.

Every theme in Pak Rat was made by someone in the community and reviewed before it was
published. You can browse them all on the web in the [Theme gallery](/themes/). To make
your own, see [Make a theme](/guide/themes/). To put yours in Pak Rat, see
[Submit a theme](/guide/submit-a-theme/).

## Choosing a theme

Go to **Settings > Appearance > Theme** and press **Left** or **Right** to
step through your themes. **None** turns themes off. The change takes effect right
away.

A theme shows in Grid view and Cover Flow only. If your **Home Layout** is **Tabs**,
change it to **Grid** or **Coverflow** in **Settings > Home Screen** to see the theme.
Menus, Settings and the Tabs layout keep your color scheme.

Leaf looks for new theme folders each time you open **Appearance**, so a theme you
copied to the card shows up without a restart.

## Browsing themes

Press the **MENU** button, open **Actions**, and choose **Pak Rat**. Press **Y** on the
list to switch between apps and themes. While you're looking at themes, the header
reads **Pak Rat Themes** and the **Y** hint reads **Apps**.

Moving through the list shows the selected theme's preview beside it, with its author
and a one-line summary. Press **X** to refresh the list, and **B** to leave Pak Rat.

Each theme in the list has a status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Available** | Not installed yet |
| **Installed** | Installed by Pak Rat, but not the theme you're using |
| **Applied** | Installed, and your current theme |
| **Update** | A newer version is out |
| **Manual** | A theme folder with the same name is already on your SD card, and Pak Rat didn't put it there |
| **Unavailable** | Pak Rat can't install this theme on your device. The details say why |

Press **A** on a theme to open its details: a large preview, the description, and its
**Author**, **License**, **Version**, the version you have installed, and the
**Folder** it installs to, such as `Themes/neon-nights`. On that page **A** does the
next step for the theme (**Install**, **Update**, **Reinstall** or **Apply**), and
**Y** uninstalls a theme Pak Rat installed.

## Installing and applying a theme

On the theme's details, press **A** to **Install**. When Pak Rat finishes, it asks
whether to apply the theme now. Choose **Apply** to switch to it, or **Not now** to
keep your current look.

To apply an installed theme later, open its details and press **A** on **Apply**, or
pick it in **Settings > Appearance > Theme**.

If the theme folder has gone missing from the card since you installed it, **A** reads
**Reinstall** and puts it back.

## Updating a theme

When a theme you installed from Pak Rat has a newer version, its status reads
**Update**. Open its details and press **A** on **Update**. If it's your current
theme, it stays applied and the new version shows up right away.

If a new version needs a newer Leaf, the details say which one, and Pak Rat keeps
offering the newest version your Leaf can use.

## Uninstalling a theme

Open the theme's details and press **Y** to **Uninstall**. Pak Rat asks first, then
deletes the theme's folder from your SD card. If it was your current theme, Grid view
and Cover Flow go back to Leaf's own look.

Pak Rat only uninstalls themes it installed. A theme you copied to the card yourself
stays until you delete its folder from `Themes` on a computer.

## A theme shows as Manual

A theme installs to a folder named after it, such as `Themes/neon-nights`. If you
already have a folder with that name that Pak Rat didn't install, for example a copy
you made by hand, the theme shows as **Manual**.

Installing it asks first, then replaces the files in that folder with the version from
the store. After that Pak Rat can update and uninstall it like any other theme. Cancel
if you want to keep your own copy.

## A theme is Unavailable

The theme's details say which of these applies:

- It was withdrawn. A theme can be taken out of Pak Rat, for example at its
  author's request. If you have it installed, it keeps working and you can still apply
  or uninstall it, but it can't be installed again or updated.
- A theme that comes with Leaf uses the same name, such as **Sample**.
- Your `Themes` folder is full. Leaf lists up to 32 themes, counting every folder in
  `Themes`: the bundled **Sample**, themes you copied yourself and the ones from Pak
  Rat. Uninstall a theme, or delete a folder you no longer use, to make room. Updating
  a theme you already have doesn't need a free slot.

## A theme won't install

- The download didn't match the catalog. Nothing was changed. Check your Wi-Fi
  and try again later.
- It isn't a valid theme. Pak Rat checks every theme before installing it and
  refuses one that fails, with a short reason. Let the Leaf team know on the
  [Leaf Discord](https://discord.gg/q5F7cZ7KRp).
