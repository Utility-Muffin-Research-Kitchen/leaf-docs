---
title: Themes from Pak Rat
description: Browse community themes in Pak Rat, preview them, and install, apply, update or remove them over Wi-Fi.
---

**Pak Rat**, Leaf's on-device store, carries themes as well as apps. You can browse
them with a preview of each one, install a theme over Wi-Fi, and wear it straight
away. Pak Rat keeps the themes it installed up to date, and removes them cleanly when
you are done with one.

Every theme in Pak Rat was made by someone in the community and reviewed before it was
published. You can browse them all on the web in the [Theme gallery](/themes/). To make
your own, see [Themes](/guide/themes/). To put yours in Pak Rat, see
[Submit a theme](/guide/submit-a-theme/).

## Browsing themes

Press the **Menu** button, open **Actions**, and choose **Pak Rat**. Press **Y** on the
list to switch between apps and themes. While you are looking at themes, the header
reads **Pak Rat Themes** and the **Y** hint reads **Apps**. Each list keeps its own
place.

Moving through the list shows the selected theme's preview beside it, with its author
and a one-line summary. Press **X** to refresh the list, and **B** to leave Pak Rat.

Each theme in the list has a status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Available** | Not installed yet |
| **Installed** | Installed by Pak Rat, but not the theme you are using |
| **Applied** | Installed, and your current theme |
| **Update** | A newer version is out |
| **Manual** | A theme folder with the same name is already on your SD card, and Pak Rat did not put it there |
| **Unavailable** | Pak Rat can't install this theme on your device. The details say why |

## Theme details

Press **A** on a theme to open its details. The page shows the status, a large
preview, the theme's description, and then its **Author**, **License**, **Version**,
the version you have installed, and the **Folder** it installs to, such as
`Themes/neon-nights`.

When something decides what you can do with the theme, a note under the preview says
so, for example that this is your current theme or that a newer version needs a newer
Leaf.

The button hints at the bottom change with the theme's state. **A** is always the
next useful thing: **Install**, **Update**, **Reinstall**, **Apply**, or **Applied**
when there is nothing left to do. **Y** is **Uninstall** for a theme Pak Rat
installed.

## Installing and applying a theme

On the theme's details, press **A** to **Install**. Pak Rat downloads the theme, checks
it, and puts it in the `Themes` folder on your SD card. When it finishes, it asks
whether to apply the theme now. Choose **Apply** to switch to it, or **Not now** to
keep your current look.

To apply an installed theme later, open its details and press **A** on **Apply**. That
is the same choice as **Settings > Appearance > Theme**, and you can switch
themes from either place.

A theme dresses Grid view and Cover Flow. If your **Home Layout** is **Tabs**, change
it to **Grid** or **Coverflow** in **Settings > Home Screen** to see the theme.

If the theme folder has gone missing from the card since you installed it, **A** reads
**Reinstall** and puts it back.

## Updating a theme

When a theme you installed from Pak Rat has a newer version, its status reads
**Update**. Open its details and press **A** on **Update**. If it is your current
theme, it stays applied and the new version shows up right away.

A new version sometimes needs a newer Leaf. The details then say which version of Leaf
it needs, and Pak Rat keeps offering the newest version your Leaf can use until you
update Leaf.

## Uninstalling a theme

Open the theme's details and press **Y** to **Uninstall**. Pak Rat asks first, then
deletes the theme's folder from your SD card.

If it is your current theme, Leaf switches to no theme, so Grid view and Cover Flow go
back to Leaf's own look. You can pick another theme at any time.

Pak Rat only uninstalls themes it installed. A theme you copied to the card yourself
stays until you delete its folder from `Themes` on a computer.

## A theme folder with the same name

A theme installs to a folder named after it, such as `Themes/neon-nights`. If you
already have a folder with that name that Pak Rat did not install, for example a copy
you made by hand, the theme shows as **Manual**.

Installing it then asks before doing anything: Pak Rat replaces the files in that
folder with the version from the store. After that, the theme belongs to Pak Rat, which
can update and uninstall it like any other. Cancel if you want to keep your own copy.

## Why a theme is Unavailable

A theme is **Unavailable** for one of these reasons, and its details say which:

- **It was withdrawn.** A theme can be taken out of Pak Rat, for example at its
  author's request. A withdrawn theme disappears from the list for anyone who has not
  installed it. If you have it installed, it stays in your list marked **Unavailable**,
  keeps working, and you can still apply or uninstall it, but it can't be installed
  again or updated.
- **A theme that comes with Leaf uses the same name.** Leaf's bundled themes, such as
  **Sample**, are replaced every time Leaf installs, so no store theme may take their
  folder name.
- **Your Themes folder is full.** Leaf lists up to 32 themes, so Pak Rat won't add a
  33rd. The count includes every folder in `Themes`, the bundled **Sample** and themes
  you copied yourself as well as the ones from Pak Rat. Uninstall a theme, or delete a
  folder you no longer use, to make room. Updating or replacing a theme you already
  have doesn't need a free slot.

## A theme won't install

- **The download did not match the catalog.** The file Pak Rat received was not the
  one that was reviewed, so nothing was changed. Try again later, and check your Wi-Fi.
- **It is not a valid theme.** Pak Rat checks every theme again before installing it
  and refuses one that fails, with a short reason. Let the Leaf team know on the
  [Leaf Discord](https://discord.gg/q5F7cZ7KRp).
