<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Runs on top of stock" icon="approve-check">
    Leaf loads alongside the stock LoongOS instead of replacing it. If anything
    goes wrong, the device falls back to stock - and a recovery card restores it
    fully.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Make it yours" icon="seti:css">
    A themeable launcher with color schemes (light and dark), list styles, nine
    fonts, an animated status bar, and an RGB-ring identity - all from the
    Settings menu.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Designed to feel good" icon="heart">
    Smooth page transitions, a consistent layout grid, readable type, and
    sensible defaults - the small touches that make Leaf pleasant to live in,
    not just configurable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Play your games" icon="rocket">
    RetroArch-powered emulation with per-system folders for ROMs, box art, and
    saves. You supply your own games and BIOS files.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apps & updates" icon="puzzle">
    Built-in apps (the Central Scrutinizer web manager, the Fugazi shader
    tuner, SSH server, file manager) and over-the-air updates straight from
    the launcher.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
