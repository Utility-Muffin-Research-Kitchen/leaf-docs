<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Runs on top of stock" icon="approve-check">
    Leaf leaves stock LoongOS installed. If Leaf fails to start, the device
    falls back to stock, and a recovery card restores it fully.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Make it yours" icon="seti:css">
    Color schemes in light and dark, list styles, nine fonts, a status bar,
    themes, and an RGB ring around the stick, all from Settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Play your games" icon="rocket">
    RetroArch emulation with one folder per system for ROMs, box art, and
    saves. You supply your own games and BIOS files.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apps & updates" icon="puzzle">
    Built-in apps (the Central Scrutinizer web manager, the Fugazi shader
    tuner, an SSH server, a file manager), more from the app store, and
    over-the-air updates from the launcher.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
