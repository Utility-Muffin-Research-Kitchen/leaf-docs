Build your own **MANGMI** colorway and see it render live.

- <a href="https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-air-x" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Air X</strong></a> - pick a body, then take the D-pad, the A/B/X/Y set, both button
  pairs, the bumpers, the triggers and the power and volume keys from any of the three
  colorways. Cap each analog stick in black or white, swap the wallpaper, and flip to
  the back for the shell and triggers.
- <a href="https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-air-y-air-y-pro" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Air Y</strong></a> and <a href="https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-air-y-air-y-pro" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Air Y Pro</strong></a> - the vertical ones, in Black and Retro GB. Mix the D-pad, the
  A/B/X/Y set, the Start/Select pair and the stick caps across the two. The Pro adds a
  second stick.
- <a href="https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-pocket-max" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Pocket Max</strong></a> - pick a body, recolor the D-pad and the A/B/X/Y set, and
  choose a wallpaper.
