Build your own **Mangmi** colorway. On the <a href="https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-air-x" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Air X</strong></a>, pick a body and then source the
D-pad, the A/B/X/Y set, both button pairs, the bumpers, the triggers and the power and
volume keys from any of the three colorways, cap each analog stick in black or white,
and swap the wallpaper - then flip to the back for the shell and triggers. The vertical
<a href="https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-air-y-air-y-pro" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Air Y</strong></a> and <a href="https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-air-y-air-y-pro" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Air Y Pro</strong></a> come in Black and Retro GB, so mix the D-pad, the
A/B/X/Y set, the Start/Select pair and the stick caps across those two - the Pro adds a
second stick. On the <a href="https://mangmi.com/products/mangmi-pocket-max" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Pocket Max</strong></a>, pick a body, recolor the D-pad and the A/B/X/Y
set, and choose a wallpaper. Mix freely and see it render live.
