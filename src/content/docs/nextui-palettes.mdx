Design a **NextUI** color palette for your TrimUI handheld. Pick a color for each UI
element with a wheel, brightness, and transparency, and watch it land live on a photo
of a **Brick** or on artwork of any **Brick**, **Brick Hammer**, **Smart Pro**, or
**Smart Pro S** colorway. Then copy the theme code or download the `.txt` to drop into
`Palettes/`.
